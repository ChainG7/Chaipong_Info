# Company

## Chaipong Engineering Co., Ltd.

Chaipong Engineering manufactures cast-iron and ductile-iron valves, water-control gates, pipe fittings, and related equipment for waterworks, irrigation, drainage, pumping, flood-control, and industrial applications.

The public website presents capabilities covering product manufacture, casting, supply, repair, and installation support. Product information references applications and requirements commonly associated with Thailand's waterworks and irrigation sectors.

## Core capabilities

- Cast-iron and ductile-iron valve manufacturing
- Sluice gate and flap valve manufacturing
- Fabricated steel and stainless-steel water-control equipment
- Pipe fittings and waterworks accessories
- Foundry production
- Repair and installation support
- Custom manufacture to approved drawings and project specifications

## Main markets

- Provincial and municipal waterworks
- Irrigation systems
- Drainage and flood protection
- Pumping stations
- Water and wastewater treatment
- Contractors and infrastructure projects
- Industrial water systems
