# Chaipong Engineering Co., Ltd. — บริษัท ชัยพงษ์เอ็นจิเนียริ่ง จำกัด

## Public company profile

The official company history states that Chaipong Engineering Co., Ltd. was established on 30 April 1996 in Bangkok. It states that the factory expanded and moved in 1999 to Sam Phran, Nakhon Pathom, where the product scope expanded to valves, waterworks equipment, water-control gates, repair, and installation services.

A public Thai business-information directory independently lists:

- registration number `0105539051385`;
- registration date 30 April 1996;
- status as operating;
- business activity covering manufacture and sale of valves, fittings, and waterworks equipment; and
- the Sam Phran address.

These third-party facts are useful corroboration but are not substitutes for an official Department of Business Development company affidavit.

## Publicly presented capabilities

- Cast-iron and ductile-iron valves
- Sluice gates and flap valves
- Fabricated-steel and stainless-steel water-control equipment
- Pipe fittings, flanges, couplings, dismantling joints, and accessories
- Foundry production for plumbing components
- Valve and pump repair and installation support
- Custom manufacture to drawings and project specifications

## Public quality and registration claims

The official website states that the company:

- operates ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 14001:2015 management systems certified by BSI Group (Thailand);
- is registered as a manufacturer for Provincial Waterworks Authority product groups; and
- publishes Thai Industrial Standards licence files for multiple valve and fitting families.

The website links to certificate and registration files in its [Download centre](https://www.chaipongengineering.com/ดาวน์โหลด/). Certificate scope, certificate number, issue date, and expiry must be checked directly from the current PDF before use in a bid or contract.

## Public research collaboration

Public research records identify Chaipong Engineering Ltd. as an affiliation or industrial facility in research involving:

- CFD investigation of molten-metal flow and solidification in plumbing components; and
- CFD analysis of a combination air-valve prototype.

These publications support a public record of industry–academic engineering activity. They do not certify every current product or manufacturing process.
