# Surface Box — หีบกุญแจ

A surface box protects access to the operating mechanism of a buried valve. It helps prevent soil from obstructing the valve operating point and allows easier operation, inspection, and maintenance.

## Selection considerations

- Required clear access to valve key or extension spindle
- Finished road or ground elevation
- Traffic/loading class
- Cover marking and locking requirements
- Corrosion protection
- Alignment with valve operating axis
