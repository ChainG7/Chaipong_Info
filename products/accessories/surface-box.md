# Surface Box — หีบกุญแจ

A surface box protects access to the operating mechanism of a buried valve and helps prevent soil from obstructing the operating point.

## Public website data

The official page states:

- Butterfly Valve application range: 400–1400 mm;
- Air Valve application range: 50–200 mm; and
- gray cast iron or ductile iron may be selected according to customer requirements.

These are `website_reference` values and must be checked against the current product catalogue, traffic/load requirement, and approved drawing.

## Selection considerations

- Valve type, operator, and required clear access
- Finished road or ground elevation
- Traffic/load class and cover seating
- Cover marking, security, and locking requirements
- Material, coating, and corrosion environment
- Alignment with the valve key or extension spindle
- Drainage and prevention of soil/debris ingress
