# Pipe Fittings and Pipeline Components

Public catalogue information identifies product families including:

- Double-flanged 45° and 90° bends
- Tapers and reducers
- Flanged sockets
- All-flanged tees
- Pipe sleeves
- Mechanical couplings
- Flanges
- Bell mouths
- Mixer cones

## Required specification fields

- Nominal diameter
- End connection and flange drilling
- Pressure class
- Material and wall thickness
- Overall and center-to-face dimensions
- Internal/external coating
- Test pressure
- Dimensional tolerance
- Applicable drawing or standard

## Evidence status

`draft` / `source-claimed` — individual product records should be created from
approved drawings and the controlled catalogue. Do not use this list as a
dimensional or procurement specification.
