# Flap Valve — ประตูระบายน้ำ

## Function

A passive non-return gate installed at an outlet or wall opening. Forward water head opens the flap; reverse water head closes it to reduce backflow.

## Product forms shown on the company website

- Cast-iron flap valve
- Fabricated galvanized-steel flap valve
- Square flap valve
- Twin-door cast-iron flap valve

The website states that a galvanized fabricated flap-valve product is manufactured with reference to Thai Industrial Standard TIS 383-2529. Applicability must be checked against the latest project specification and current standard status.

## Typical applications

- Drainage outlets
- Pump discharge structures
- Flood-protection systems
- Canal and river outfalls
- Protection against reverse flow, debris, or animal entry, where suitable

## Critical design checks

- Forward head needed to open the flap
- Maximum reverse head
- Hinge and pin loading
- Bush and bearing material
- Debris, sand, and sediment exposure
- Seal and seating geometry
- Corrosion and galvanizing/coating system
- Access for inspection and cleaning

## Maintenance risks

Sand or abrasive debris entering hinge and bush interfaces can accelerate wear. Design should provide suitable clearances, bearing materials, sealing or shielding where practical, drainage, and accessible periodic inspection.
