# Flap Valve — ประตูระบายน้ำ

## Function

A passive non-return gate installed at an outlet or wall opening. Forward head opens the flap; reverse head closes it to reduce backflow.

The public page describes flood/backflow protection and reducing animal entry into drains. It lists related cast-iron, fabricated-steel, square, and twin-door forms across the website.

## Critical design checks

- Forward head required to open the flap
- Maximum reverse head and structural load
- Hinge, pin, bush, and bearing loads/materials
- Debris, sand, sediment, and marine exposure
- Seal and seating geometry
- Corrosion, galvanizing, and coating system
- Safe inspection and cleaning access
- Counterweight or hydraulic behavior, where provided
