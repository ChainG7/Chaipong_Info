# Sluice Gate — ประตูน้ำบานเลื่อน

## Function

A vertically moving gate used to isolate or regulate flow through wall openings, channels, chambers, reservoirs, pumping stations, and treatment structures.

## Construction referenced by the company website

- Cast-iron sluice gates
- Fabricated-steel gates
- Stainless-steel gates
- Custom configurations to project requirements

The website references cast-iron sluice gates associated with AWWA C501 and AWWA C560. The applicable edition and full compliance requirements must be confirmed for each project.

## Key engineering terms

- **Seating head:** pressure pushes the gate toward its frame or seat.
- **Off-seating head:** pressure tends to push the gate away from its frame or seat.
- **Unseating force:** force required to overcome water load, seal friction, stem friction, and mechanical resistance.
- **Flush bottom:** bottom arrangement intended to reduce obstruction or sediment trapping at the sill.

## Information required before design or quotation

- Clear opening: width × height
- Design water head on each side
- Seating and off-seating conditions
- Wall, channel, or embedded mounting
- Operating floor elevation
- Stem length and number of guides
- Manual, gearbox, electric, or hydraulic operation
- Material and corrosion-protection requirements
- Leakage acceptance and test method
