# Air Valve — ประตูระบายอากาศ

Air valves release accumulated air during operation and/or admit air during pipeline draining or negative-pressure conditions, depending on design.

## Common types

- Single air-release valve
- Air/vacuum valve
- Combination air valve

## Selection factors

- Pipeline profile and high points
- Filling and draining rate
- Pump trip and surge conditions
- Working pressure
- Required air-flow capacity
- Water quality and contamination risk
- Isolation and maintenance access
