# Air Valve — ประตูระบายอากาศ

## Function

Air valves release accumulated air and/or admit air during filling, draining, pump-trip, or negative-pressure events, depending on the valve type. Air-valve sizing requires pipeline-profile and transient analysis; nominal pipe diameter alone is insufficient.

## Public product families

The official website and download centre list:

- Single Air Valve
- Double Air Valve
- High-Speed Air Valve
- Combination Air Valve

The download centre links a TIS 1368-2539 licence file and current maintenance manuals for several air-valve types. Scope, model, size, pressure, and validity must be checked directly.

## Selection factors

- Pipeline profile and high points
- Filling and draining rates
- Pump-trip and surge conditions
- Working and transient pressure
- Required air-discharge and air-intake capacity
- Orifice behavior under differential pressure
- Water quality, debris, and contamination risk
- Isolation valve and safe maintenance access

## Research context

Mahidol University publishes a research record for CFD analysis of a combination air-valve prototype. The research record supports public evidence of technical investigation; it does not certify all commercial models.
