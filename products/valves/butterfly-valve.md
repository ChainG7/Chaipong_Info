# Butterfly Valve — ประตูน้ำลิ้นปีกผีเสื้อ

## Function

A quarter-turn valve used to isolate or regulate water flow in a pipeline. A circular disc rotates within the valve body between open and closed positions.

## Typical applications

- Water transmission and distribution
- Pumping stations
- Water and wastewater treatment
- Irrigation pipelines
- Industrial water systems

## Engineering information to confirm per project

- Nominal diameter and face-to-face dimension
- Pressure class and design pressure
- Body and disc material
- Seat type and allowable leakage
- Flange drilling standard
- Manual gear, electric actuator, or other operator
- Required opening/closing torque
- Coating and corrosion-protection system
- Hydrostatic and seat testing requirements

## Notes

Do not select an actuator or gearbox from nominal valve size alone. Differential pressure, seat design, bearing condition, flow velocity, operating frequency, and safety factor affect required torque.
