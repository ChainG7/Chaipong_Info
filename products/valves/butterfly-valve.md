# Butterfly Valve — ประตูน้ำลิ้นปีกผีเสื้อ

## Function

A quarter-turn valve used for pipeline isolation and, where the selected design and operating conditions permit, flow regulation. The company page describes manual gearbox, electric, pneumatic, and hydraulic operation, with rubber-seat and metal-seat forms.

## Typical applications

- Water transmission and distribution
- Pumping stations
- Water and wastewater treatment
- Irrigation pipelines
- Industrial water systems

## Project checks

- Nominal diameter, face-to-face dimension, and flange drilling
- Pressure class and maximum differential pressure
- Body, disc, shaft, seat, bearing, and fastener materials
- Allowable leakage and test method
- Required operating torque and actuator duty
- Coating/lining and potable-water requirements
- Flow velocity, surge, cavitation, and throttling limits
