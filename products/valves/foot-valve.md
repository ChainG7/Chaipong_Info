# Foot Valve — หัวกะโหลกทางดูด

## Function

A non-return valve installed at a pump suction inlet to retain prime in the suction line. The public page describes an inlet screen intended to reduce entry of debris.

## Installation and maintenance notes from the public manual

The company manual instructs users to install the valve vertically, clean the interior and seating surfaces before installation, tighten flange bolts in a balanced sequence, and test for leakage. It also recommends periodic screen and seating-surface cleaning.

Maintenance intervals in a public manual are general source claims, not universal project requirements. Confirm water quality, debris loading, duty cycle, accessibility, and approved maintenance instructions.
