# Gate Valve — ประตูน้ำลิ้นยก

A linear-motion isolation valve in which a gate moves perpendicular to the flow path. It is generally intended for fully open or fully closed service rather than continuous throttling.

## Typical applications

- Water-distribution mains
- Pumping stations
- Treatment plants
- Isolation points in buried or above-ground pipelines

## Project data required

- Valve type and closure design
- Nominal diameter and pressure class
- Flange or socket interface
- Body, gate, stem, and seat materials
- Stem arrangement: rising or non-rising
- Operating method and extension requirements
- Coating, lining, testing, and leakage criteria
