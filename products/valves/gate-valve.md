# Gate Valve — ประตูน้ำลิ้นยก

## Function

A linear-motion isolation valve whose gate moves perpendicular to the flow path. The official page describes it as primarily intended for fully open or fully closed service, not continuous throttling.

## Selection notes

- Use full-open/full-closed service unless the approved design explicitly permits throttling.
- Confirm metal-seated versus resilient-seated construction.
- Confirm buried-service access, extension spindle, and surface-box arrangement.
- Verify flange drilling, face-to-face dimensions, coating, leakage class, and test pressure.
