# Product Selection Guide

## 1. Define the hydraulic function

Choose whether the equipment must isolate, regulate, prevent reverse flow, release air, control an open channel, connect piping, or support the pipeline.

## 2. Define operating conditions

- Medium and water quality
- Normal and maximum pressure/head
- Differential pressure at operation
- Flow rate and velocity
- Temperature
- Frequency and speed of operation
- Reverse-flow or surge conditions
- Sand, debris, corrosion, and immersion exposure

## 3. Define interface conditions

- Pipe size and flange drilling
- Clear wall opening
- Wall/chamber/channel geometry
- Available installation and maintenance space
- Operating floor elevation
- Buried, submerged, indoor, or outdoor service

## 4. Define performance requirements

- Allowable leakage
- Required closing/opening time
- Manual or powered operation
- Fail position and emergency operation
- Design life and maintenance interval
- Coating, lining, galvanizing, or stainless-steel requirements

## 5. Confirm controlled documents

Selection is complete only after confirming the approved datasheet, drawing, specification, standard edition, inspection plan, and acceptance criteria.
