# Installation and Maintenance Framework

Status: `reviewed-framework` — general guidance only. The approved project drawing,
method statement, safety plan, and product-specific manual always take precedence.

## Before installation

- Verify product tag, size, materials, direction, and approved drawing.
- Inspect for transport damage, coating damage, contamination, and loose parts.
- Confirm civil dimensions, level, flatness, anchor locations, and clearances.
- Protect seats, seals, stems, bearings, and machined surfaces from welding debris and grout.

## During installation

- Lift only from approved lifting points.
- Align without forcing the product to correct pipe or civil misalignment.
- Tighten anchors and flange bolts in a controlled sequence.
- Use specified grout, gaskets, lubricants, and fastener torque.
- Check free movement before final commissioning.

## Commissioning

- Clean the system and remove construction debris.
- Operate through the complete travel range.
- Check direction, limit switches, torque settings, and manual override.
- Conduct leakage and functional tests using approved procedures.
- Record results and close all punch-list items.

## Routine maintenance

- Inspect coating, corrosion, leakage, fasteners, seals, hinges, bushes, stems, guides, and operators.
- Remove sand, silt, weeds, and debris.
- Lubricate only where the approved manual requires it.
- Record operating torque or abnormal resistance trends.
- Replace worn parts before structural damage occurs.

## Safety

Apply site lockout/tagout, confined-space, lifting, stored-energy, pressure-isolation, and water-control procedures. This framework does not replace the project safety plan or product manual.
