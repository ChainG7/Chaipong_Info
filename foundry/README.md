# Foundry Knowledge

The company website presents casting products and categories for cast iron and ductile iron, including grades listed as iron grades 15, 20, 25, 30 and FCD.

## Intended scope

This section should contain controlled summaries for:

- Casting materials and grade equivalence
- Chemical and mechanical requirements
- Melting and inoculation practice
- CO₂ sodium-silicate moulding process
- Pattern and core control
- Heat/lot traceability
- Casting inspection and test plans
- Defect classification and corrective action
- Machining allowance and dimensional control
- Coating preparation

## Material-control rule

Do not infer grade equivalence from trade names alone. Each record should identify the governing standard, grade designation, mechanical properties, chemical limitations where applicable, sampling method, and acceptance evidence.
