## Rule for product pages

A product page may record a standard as:

- `website_reference` when only the public page states it;
- `certificate_linked` when the official download centre links a licence/certificate;
- `controlled_verified` only after a named reviewer checks the current controlled document and its scope.

## Copyright

Store bibliographic references and original company summaries only. Do not commit unauthorized copies, scans, or substantial extracts of standards.
