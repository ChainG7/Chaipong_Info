## What this repository is

Chaipong Engineering Co., Ltd. presents itself as a Thai manufacturer and supplier of waterworks valves, water-control gates, pipe fittings, and related equipment. The company website states that the business was established on 30 April 1996 and later expanded its factory to Sam Phran, Nakhon Pathom.

This repository transforms public product pages, catalogues, manuals, certificates, and external public records into structured engineering notes. It is designed for human navigation, technical review, search, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

## Content trust model

Every technical record must declare a status:

| Status | Meaning | Permitted use |
|---|---|---|
| `verified` | Checked against a current approved drawing, specification, certificate, or controlled company document | May be used within the scope stated in the record |
| `reviewed` | Technically reviewed, but controlled evidence or project applicability is incomplete | General technical reference only |
| `draft` | Structured from public or preliminary information | Not for quotation, fabrication, procurement, or contractual use |
| `archived` | Retained for history and superseded | Historical reference only |

Public website claims are recorded as `source-claimed` evidence. A search result, business directory, or third-party page can corroborate a fact but cannot make a product specification `verified`.

## Important engineering notice

This repository does not replace approved drawings, purchase specifications, project requirements, certified standards, inspection records, or formal engineering calculations. Size ranges, pressure classes, materials, leakage limits, actuator torque, coating systems, and standard editions must be confirmed for each contract and project.

Do not upload confidential drawings, customer information, prices, credentials, or unauthorized copies of copyrighted standards.
